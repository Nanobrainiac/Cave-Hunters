# Decisions

Approved: local shared-screen/mouse multiplayer, 2–6 players, top-down 2D, TypeScript/Vite/SVG/CSS, local loopback hosting, no accounts/backend. User agreed to v0.3 direction.

Adopted prototype defaults from agreed direction, subject to playtest: public equipment and Lantern discoveries; six copies per trap and four per item; same-room Rusty Blade targeting, occupied nodes block forced paths, resolve each step and revalidate; skip placement if full, cancel drop if no destination; retry failed room draw from same connection consuming normal move. Exit design must support six starts and winning spot; exact geometry pending.

Still open: exact room templates and Exit layout; precise item timing and skip-turn precedence; permanent blocking; reachability definition and multi-edge placement conflicts. Do not silently encode unresolved rules as final.

Phase 2 prototype: Exit uses a 300×300 square, six starts on a ring, central winning spot, four midpoint edge ports, 11 nodes and 14 links. Layout remains subject to playtesting. Player count selector is a tabletop preview, not match setup.

Phase 4 prototype: 30 room cards use midpoint edge ports and sparse/dense graph variants. Adjacent aligned ports are distinct linked nodes, visually inset to allow clicking each; unmatched neighboring edges are closed. Reachability ignores temporary player blocking because all placed room graphs connect to the existing cave. All current cards can rotate to fit any free edge; discard handling exists for future incompatible templates. Exact room geometry is provisional.

Phase 5: six copies of each trap, four of each equipment, four relics; identity concealed until encounter. Automatic die on successful placement; mandatory blind seeding, capped by eligible spots and supply. Late-phase seeding starts on subsequent turns. Trap encounters reveal and consume traps without effects until Phase 6; equipment collected but not usable yet. Victory deferred to Phase 7.

Phase 6 prototype: equipment usable before or after movement outside mandatory placement/effect decisions. Snare blocks movement and equipment; mandatory late seeding still occurs. Blade stops at any trap encounter (including canceled traps), may stop after one step, and does not expand rooms. Drop paths treat players as blockers, but board tokens only block landing. Public revealed tokens stay on board. Victory deferred to Phase 7.

User-approved Possession revision (v0.6.2): replace delayed next-turn movement selection with immediate forced movement by the player to the left, one to four legal linked steps, selecting a destination from all reachable spots with one click (v0.6.3). Automatic shortest route, stable board-link order for ties, independent of hidden token identities. Like Blade, it resolves each encounter and ends the displacement on a new trap; no room discovery. Protective Charm can cancel it. This supersedes the original v0.2 rule. Shared mouse is handed to the decision owner; no identity enforcement.

Token spawning revision (v0.6.6): on every successful room placement, automatically draw one or two hidden tokens (50/50), take them from the shuffled remaining supply, and uniformly pick distinct available nodes across the entire existing board, including Exit and the new room. Exclude players and existing tokens, cap by available supply/spots. Manual player placement is removed. Preserve late-phase one-token-per-turn spawning but make that automatic. This supersedes the Phase 5 die distribution [0,0,1,1,2,2], mandatory manual seeding, and the turn-state placement gate.

Phase 7 prototype defaults (v0.7.0; subject to playtest): any settled movement onto the original winning node with at least two relics qualifies, including forced movement out of turn. Forced routes stop on victory. A player already on Exit who acquires a second relic by theft also wins. Trap reactions resolve before qualification, so losing a relic or being displaced can prevent a win. Local autosave uses one versioned slot per browser origin, retains pending gameplay effects and restores a canonical validated board; camera, selections and informational dialogs reset. Restart and replacing a save require confirmation. No new stalemate/draw rule is introduced.
