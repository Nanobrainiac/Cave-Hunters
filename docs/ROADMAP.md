# Roadmap

1. Foundation — repository, documentation, TypeScript/Vite entry point, tests, Windows launcher. Verify install/build/tests locally before marking complete.
2. Virtual tabletop — Exit room SVG, nodes, pieces, pan/zoom.
3. Local multiplayer — setup, turn rotation, legal one-step movement, occupancy.
4. Exploration — deck, room rotation, placement and graph expansion.
5. Tokens — hidden supply, die, placement, encounters, relics.
6. Effects — traps, items, reactions, forced movement and drops.
7. Full loop — victory, late phase, saves, restart.
8. Polish — artwork, sound, tests, balancing and local distribution.
